## General Information

**Passenger Volume Prediction** forecasts passenger demand from real-world transportation data, giving operational teams a basis for planning capacity and allocating resources ahead of demand rather than reacting to it.

**My Role:** AI Engineer - feature engineering, model development, and evaluation.

**Project Overview:**

**1. Real-World Operational Data:**

Built predictive models on real transportation data from DB Regio, working with the irregularities of production data rather than a curated benchmark set.

**2. Temporal and Seasonal Feature Engineering:**

Engineered temporal and seasonal features to improve model accuracy and robustness, capturing the recurring cycles that drive passenger demand.

**3. Demand Forecasting:**

Applied machine learning models to predict passenger volume, producing forecasts intended to support operational planning and resource allocation.

## Project Description

**1. Data Foundation:**

**Source Data:**

- Real-world transportation data from DB Regio as the basis for modelling.

- Operational passenger records rather than a synthetic or benchmark dataset.

- Preparation and cleaning to make production data suitable for supervised learning.

---

**2. Feature Engineering:**

**Temporal Features:**

- Time-based features capturing patterns tied to when travel occurs.

- Derived signals intended to expose recurring structure in the demand series.

**Seasonal Features:**

- Seasonal components engineered to represent longer-cycle variation in passenger volume.

- Feature work targeted at improving both accuracy and robustness of the resulting models.

---

**3. Modelling:**

**Predictive Models:**

- Machine learning models trained to forecast passenger demand.

- Time series framing of the prediction problem, respecting the temporal ordering of observations.

- Model evaluation focused on forecast reliability across varying conditions.

---

**4. Operational Application:**

**Planning Support:**

- Forecasts positioned to inform operational planning decisions.

- Demand predictions intended to guide resource allocation ahead of observed volume.
